Daily loop

Capture. Sort. Notice.

Three steps. Fifteen seconds. No prompts to perform wellness or rate your mood on a scale of one to ten.

01
Capture

One sentence. Voice or text. No blank-page overwhelm, no prompts, no minimum length. Skip is always fine.

02
Sort

Green (what rose, what held) or red (what fell, what cost you). Mixed when both. AI may suggest — you always decide.

03
Notice

At most one pattern per week. Asymmetry, contradiction, drift — stated as fact. Then silence. No advice.

What it's not

Engel refuses the usual journal app posture.

—Not a productivity app or task manager
—Not a habit tracker or streak machine
—Not a mood logger or wellness journal
—Not a therapist, coach, or advice engine
—No scores, no guilt loops, no nudges
—Never: journey, healing, you got this

Voice

Honest. Quiet. Non-motivational.

Second person. States data. Names the pattern, then stops.
