## Brand aesthetic (paste into Pomelli — one per line, press Enter)

Copy each line into **Brand Aesthetic** separately:

```
Near-black room (#0c0c0e)
Warm cream ink (#ECE7DD) on dark
Two soft glowing fields — green and red, not 3D spheres
Bioluminescent light — feathered edges, no rim highlights
Fraunces serif + JetBrains Mono
Sparse, editorial layout — one idea per frame
Club intensity meets private grief — Hardstyle 2 register
Tough love inverted — you were already working hard enough
Cathartic recognition — precise enough to make you cry, not cheer
ADHD survival without explaining ADHD
Repetition as labor, not laziness
Honest, quiet, non-motivational
No hustle culture, no wellness pastels, no streak badges
Body under pressure — breath, buck, burn, then stillness
Green = energy / wins / aliveness
Red = friction / heaviness / stuck
One sentence, fifteen seconds — low friction as respect
```

## Three campaign prompts (run first)

1. A quiet campaign for people with ADHD who know their patterns but can't break them — you've been working hard enough; Engel holds the record, no motivation

2. Show the moment of sorting a journal entry into green or red — the physical act of deciding, body still burning from the week

3. For people who open a journaling app, feel overwhelmed, and close it — Engel is different because it only asks for one sentence; another breath you can actually take

4. *Hardstyle 2 register:* "Tough love, you're not working hard enough" — but inverted: campaign shows the fragments that prove they were

Save outputs you like back into Business DNA so it compounds.
